AP=Arnold Palmer
AP normally gives 200 items in an exploring zone. You get 500 items when you have the the Lemon Squeezer Farm Supply perk.
The Quandary Chowder  meal makes lemonade and AP 10% more effective for 5 minutes giving you a total of 550 items with the perk.
Using a Lemon Cream Pie meal will allow you to use 5x Arnold Palmer per click to explore faster.
The Lemon Seltzer meal makes your arnold palmer give 50% more items for 50 uses when exploring and stacks multiplicatively with Quandary Chowder. This also works with Lemon Cream Pie with 50 actions meaning you get this bonus on up to 250 Arnold Palmer uses for an effective increase of 125 bonus Arnold Palmer worth of items.
