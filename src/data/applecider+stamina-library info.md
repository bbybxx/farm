## Apple Cider and Exploring Effectiveness

For every point added to exploring effectiveness, you explore 12.5 more times when using apple cider. (This number is rounded)

So if you are exploring 24 times per click in an area and you pay the fee to increase it your exploring effectiveness, (with both Sprint Shoes I and II perks) you will now get 28 explores per click. This brings the extra explores you get with cider from 300 to 350 (12.5 x 4 = 50).

The base number of explores is 1000 so you add thatnow you can add either 1000 or 1250 to that for your new total depending on whether or not you have the Cinnamon Sticks perk.

## Apple Cider

Explore faster! Requires stamina.

Apple cider Apple Cider is a click-reducing item that allows you to explore many times with a single click. Without any perks or upgrades it explores 1010 times and cannot be used unless you have at least 1010 stamina, but this number can be increased with perks and Exploring Effectiveness upgrades.
To help offset its crafting cost of 40 apples Apple, cider also increases drop rates in most zones.

Perks and Meals
Apple cider benefits from a number of perks and meal effects:
Cinnamon Sticks increases your cider explore count by 25%, to a minimum of 1263. This also increases the minimum stamina required to use a cider and the average stamina consumed when using one.
Wanderer perks reduce the amount of stamina used by cider in the same way that they reduce stamina use when manually exploring, up to a maximum of 33%.
Sprint Shoes perks increase your exploring effectiveness, which in turn increases your cider explore count. See below for more info.
Neigh Neigh decreases the stamina used when exploring with cider by 20%, stacking multiplicatively with Wanderer for a maximum reduction of 46.4%.
Cabbage stew Cabbage Stew allows you to use 5 ciders per click, speeding up exploration even more.

Drop Rate Buff
When exploring manually, the zones around Redbrook have different Zone Drop Rates. These rates determine how many items you find (on average) each time you explore. These range from 0.25 (1 item for every 4 explores) to 0.40 (1 item for every 2.5 explores). Cider sets this value to 0.40 in every zone.

This is usually a buff, and has the largest effect on zones with lower zone drop rates like Highland Hills, Whispering Creek, and Jundland Desert.

Exploring Effectiveness
Your Exploring Effectiveness in each zone also increases your cider explore count. Before any perks or upgrades, each point of exploring effectiveness will grant an additional 10 explores. With Cinnamon Sticks, this increases to 12.5 explores (rounded as needed). This also increases the minimum stamina required to use a cider, and the average stamina consumed when using one.

## Exploring Effectiveness
In any exploration location, click on the Info button.



Here, one of the Townsfolk has the option to help you explore more effectively. You will now explore more times when you tap. This means for each point of exploring effectiveness you have, you explore an equal amount of times when you tap, thus reducing the amount of taps needed to explore. Your item discovery chance will stay the same, allowing you to possibly find multiple items per tap.





Each upgrade costs 10x more silver/corn than the previous upgrade. When you explore, you will use the listed amount of stamina for the area. Each area must be upgraded separately.

Wanderer perks still save stamina. When you have less stamina left than your explore allows, only 1 stamina is used per explore.

You can use a Protein Bar in an area to increase the exploring effectiveness without increasing the silver/corn cost. So if your next upgrade would cost 1b and you use the protein bar, your exploring effectiveness will go up by 1 (more if you have perks) and your next upgrade will still cost 1b. 3 Protein Bar can be used on any given exploration location for free (plus the cost of the protein bar). After you have used 3 Protein Bar in a location, future protein bar uses cost an increasing amount of gold starting at 25 and going up by 25 each time (25, 50, 75, etc...).

Apple Cider takes into account your Exploring Effectiveness in an Explore location. See Apple Cider for more info and a handy chart.

The Sprint Shoes I perk, from Farm Supply, doubles the number of explores gained from upgrading your exploring effectiveness. - Example: If your effectiveness in an area is 4, with Sprint Shoes that becomes 8. Sprint shoes II doubles that again, so if you have both perks, your 4 would actually be 16.

This page contains information about the exploring mechanic. The data below was sourced from Buddy Farm.

Apple Cider
Apple cider sets the drop rate to 0.40 regardless of zone. This is usually a buff, and means that cider is more effective (relative to manual exploration) in zones with lower base drop rates. This is only particularly relevant when deciding whether to craft/trade for cider or eat apples and explore manually.
